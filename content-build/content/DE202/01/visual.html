3. The Backfill Rescue

A transform bug started silently corrupting click_position on July 3. Drag to the day you notice. The damage grows either way — what changes is whether it is reversible. Like a receipt drawer: keep it and any mistake is re-addable.

This is the whole argument for the Raw zone in one picture: with it, recovery cost is flat — one backfill query, no matter how late you look. Without it, the cost is the damage itself, and it grows every day you do not notice.
