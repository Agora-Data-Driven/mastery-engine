3. Where Slices Land

Monday's load failed. You replay it on Wednesday. Press replay and watch where the rows go under each partitioning scheme. Like filing receipts by the date printed on them versus the date you happened to open the envelope.

Deterministic means the partition is a pure function of the element's own data. Ingestion date is a function of when the job ran — so the output changes depending on when you run it, and the overwrite from visual 2 has no correct slice to truncate.

4. The Scan Bill

2 TB of history, growing 5 GB/day. Drag the retention and watch what a daily job scans over a year each way. Like re-reading the whole book every night to find out what changed on the last page.

Full refresh
Incremental

Incremental wins on cost and loses on safety: full refresh is self-healing — whatever was wrong yesterday is rebuilt from scratch today. That is why the professional default for a large, important table is hybrid: incremental daily, scheduled full reconciliation as the audit.
