1. Rebuild Beside And Swap

Single Source of Truth vs Derived Copies said which copy is authoritative, and Freshness SLAs and Lag Metrics said the gap between snapshot and publish is measurable. A rebuild spends both at once. Choose how v4 gets built, then decide whether to replay the gap before the alias moves. Like repainting a shop: you can close and paint the room you are standing in, or fit out the room next door and move the sign across in one go.

A generation is the whole tuple — embedding model, chunker version, quantization config and index build — tagged together, not just "the index". That is what lets every metric and every reconciliation count attribute to exactly one system state, which is the difference between "quality dropped" and "quality dropped on v4".

2. The Four Gates

Validation before cutover is four gates in a fixed order, cheapest first. Switch gates off and watch which defects walk straight past you and out to users. Like airport security: bag scan, metal detector, pat-down, watch-list — skip one and you learn which one it was from the passengers.

Sign-off is written: every gate has an owner, a threshold and a recorded result. That is what makes the cutover meeting a formality when the data is green and a no-go when it is not — instead of a debate about whether the numbers feel acceptable, which is a debate the person who did the work always wins.

3. Sizing The Shadow

A shadow is only evidence if it contains the query class you are afraid of, for long enough to cover a traffic cycle. Move the three dials and watch how often the exact-ID class — three percent of traffic — actually gets tested. Like a food survey: asking twelve people tells you nothing about the rare allergy, however carefully you write the questionnaire.

Results are compared continuously, not merely collected. Overlap and score-distribution deltas are computed as the soak runs, so a degradation visible at hour three stops the soak at hour three — rather than being read about on Friday, after the swap.

4. Full Or Incremental

The backfill type follows the blast radius of the change, and it is decided in writing before the work starts. Tap a change to see what it touches and what it costs — then re-run the job to see why replay has to be idempotent. Like a recipe change: new flour means baking everything again, a new label on the jar does not.

Completion is a verified count match from reconciliation, not a job exit code — finished is a state of the data, not of the scheduler. And the whole job runs as scavenger traffic: serving is the foreground, the backfill takes whatever the dashboards say is spare.

5. Rollback And Mixing

Rollback is a rehearsed alias move — until the window closes, at which point the same symptom has a completely different answer. And while two generations coexist, some mixes are harmless and some are quietly wrong. Like keeping the old key to a flat you have moved out of: it costs nothing for a month, and after the locks change you are calling a locksmith.

The rehearsed incident: Thursday 10:20 the alias moves to v4; 11:05 canaries show the exact-ID class down nine percent; 11:10 the pre-agreed threshold makes the decision; 11:12 the alias is back on v3 and caches are invalidated; 11:20 verification is green. Forty-two minutes of degraded behaviour instead of a day of it — in an incident you do not rise to the occasion, you fall to the level of your rehearsal.
