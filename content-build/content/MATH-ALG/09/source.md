### Standard and expanded equations of a circle

- Q: In the standard form $(x-h)^2 + (y-k)^2 = r^2$ of a circle, which features are read off directly?
    [ ] The $x$-intercept and $y$-intercept
    [ ] The area and the circumference
    [ ] The slope and the $y$-intercept
    [CORRECT] The center and the radius
- Q: Expand $(x-2)^2 + (y+1)^2 = 9$ into general form.
    [ ] $x^2 + y^2 - 4x + 2y - 4 = 9$
    [ ] $x^2 + y^2 - 2x + y = 9$
    [CORRECT] $x^2 + y^2 - 4x + 2y - 4 = 0$
    [ ] $x^2 + y^2 + 4x - 2y - 4 = 0$
- Q: What is the radius of the circle $(x+1)^2 + (y-4)^2 = 25$?
    [ ] 12.5
    [ ] 4
    [ ] 25
    [CORRECT] 5
- Q: What is the center of the circle $(x-3)^2 + (y+2)^2 = 16$?
    [CORRECT] (3,-2)
    [ ] (-3,2)
    [ ] (-3,-2)
    [ ] (3,2)
- Q: Why is $x^2 + y^2 + 6x - 8y + 9 = 0$ not yet written in the standard form $(x-h)^2 + (y-k)^2 = r^2$?
    [ ] Because the radius that it implies would come out as negative
    [ ] Because the equation is missing a required constant term
    [CORRECT] Because its x and y terms are not yet completed into squares
    [ ] Because a true circle equation cannot contain any linear terms
