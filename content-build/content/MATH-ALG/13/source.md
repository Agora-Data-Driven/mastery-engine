### Rational exponents and properties

- Q: Simplify the expression $(x^{2/3})^3$.
    [ ] $x^{5/3}$
    [ ] $x^6$
    [ ] $x$
    [CORRECT] $x^2$
- Q: Rewrite $\frac{1}{x^{1/2}}$ using radical notation.
    [ ] $\sqrt{x}$
    [ ] $x\sqrt{x}$
    [CORRECT] $\frac{1}{\sqrt{x}}$
    [ ] $\sqrt{\frac{1}{x^2}}$
- Q: For $x \ge 0$, what does the expression $x^{1/2}$ represent?
    [CORRECT] $\sqrt{x}$, the square root of $x$
    [ ] $2x$, the value $x$ doubled
    [ ] $x^2$, the square of $x$
    [ ] $\frac{1}{2x}$, the reciprocal of $2x$
- Q: Which exponent rule is valid for all positive bases a and b and real exponents?
    [ ] $(a^m)^n = a^{m+n}$
    [CORRECT] $(ab)^r = a^r \cdot b^r$
    [ ] $a^{m+n} = a^m + a^n$
    [ ] $\frac{a^m}{a^n} = a^{mn}$
- Q: Simplify the product $x^{1/2} \cdot x^{3/2}$.
    [ ] $x$
    [ ] $x^{3/4}$
    [CORRECT] $x^2$
    [ ] $x^3$
