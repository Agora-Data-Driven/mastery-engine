### Introduction to ellipses and hyperbolas

- Q: A hyperbola is defined as the set of all points for which
    [ ] the product of distances to two fixed points is constant
    [ ] the distance to one fixed point stays constant
    [ ] the sum of distances to two fixed points is constant
    [CORRECT] the difference of distances to two fixed points is constant
- Q: An ellipse is defined as the set of all points for which
    [ ] the distance to one fixed point stays constant
    [CORRECT] the sum of distances to two fixed points is constant
    [ ] the difference of distances to two fixed points is constant
    [ ] the product of distances to two fixed points is constant
- Q: Which equation represents an ellipse centered at the origin?
    [CORRECT] $\frac{x^2}{9} + \frac{y^2}{4} = 1$
    [ ] $\frac{x^2}{9} - \frac{y^2}{4} = 1$
    [ ] $y = x^2 + 9$
    [ ] $x^2 + y^2 = 9$
- Q: Which equation represents a hyperbola centered at the origin?
    [ ] $\frac{x^2}{9} + \frac{y^2}{4} = 1$
    [ ] $x^2 + y^2 = 16$
    [ ] $y = x^2 - 16$
    [CORRECT] $\frac{x^2}{9} - \frac{y^2}{4} = 1$
- Q: For the ellipse $\frac{x^2}{16} + \frac{y^2}{9} = 1$, the major axis is oriented
    [ ] diagonally
    [ ] vertically
    [CORRECT] horizontally
    [ ] in no fixed direction

### Foci, vertices, and equations of conics

- Q: For the ellipse $\frac{x^2}{25} + \frac{y^2}{9} = 1$, what are the vertices on the major axis?
    [ ] $(0, \pm 5)$
    [ ] $(\pm 3, 0)$
    [CORRECT] $(\pm 5, 0)$
    [ ] $(0, \pm 3)$
- Q: For the ellipse $\frac{x^2}{25} + \frac{y^2}{9} = 1$, find the distance $c$ from the center to each focus.
    [ ] $c = 3$
    [ ] $c = 5$
    [ ] $c = 2$
    [CORRECT] $c = 4$
- Q: For an ellipse centered at the origin with a horizontal major axis, which relationship holds among $a$, $b$, and $c$?
    [ ] $a = b$, with $c^2 = a^2 + b^2$
    [ ] $a < b$, with $c^2 = b^2 - a^2$
    [CORRECT] $a > b$, with $c^2 = a^2 - b^2$
    [ ] $c > a$, with $c^2 = a^2 + b^2$
- Q: For the hyperbola $\frac{x^2}{9} - \frac{y^2}{4} = 1$, what are the coordinates of the vertices?
    [ ] $(\pm 2, 0)$
    [ ] $(0, \pm 3)$
    [CORRECT] $(\pm 3, 0)$
    [ ] $(0, \pm 2)$
- Q: For an ellipse, where $a$ is the semi-major axis length, $b$ is the semi-minor axis length, and $c$ is the distance from the center to a focus, which of the following is always true?
    [ ] $a < b$, and $c^2 = b^2 - a^2$ is the formula for $c$.
    [ ] $a = b$, and $c$ would be $0$, defining a circular shape.
    [CORRECT] $a > b$, and $c^2 = a^2 - b^2$ is the formula for $c$.
    [ ] $c > a$, and $c^2 = a^2 + b^2$ is the formula for $c$.
- Q: For the hyperbola $\frac{x^2}{16} - \frac{y^2}{9} = 1$, find the distance $c$ from the center to each focus.
    [ ] $c = 4$
    [ ] $c = 25$
    [CORRECT] $c = 5$
    [ ] $c = 7$
