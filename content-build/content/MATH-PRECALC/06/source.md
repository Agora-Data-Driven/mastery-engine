### Vector components, magnitude, and direction

- Q: Express the vector with magnitude 6 and direction 0° (along the positive x-axis) in component form.
    [CORRECT] <6, 0>
    [ ] <-6, 0>
    [ ] <0, -6>
    [ ] <0, 6>
- Q: For a vector $\langle a, b \rangle$, which expression gives its magnitude?
    [ ] $a + b$
    [CORRECT] $\sqrt{a^2 + b^2}$
    [ ] a
    [ ] +
    [ ] b
    [ ] $a^2 + b^2$
- Q: How can a vector in the coordinate plane be represented?
    [ ] By a single real number giving its length
    [ ] By a 2x2 matrix of its endpoints
    [CORRECT] By an ordered pair of components, such as <a, b>
    [ ] By one complex number's modulus alone
- Q: Which vector points in the direction 90° measured counterclockwise from the positive x-axis?
    [ ] <0, -1>
    [ ] <-1, 0>
    [ ] <1, 0>
    [CORRECT] <0, 1>
- Q: Find the magnitude of the vector <3, 4>.
    [ ] 7
    [ ] 25
    [ ] 12
    [CORRECT] 5

### Scalar multiplication, addition, and subtraction

- Q: What is the effect of multiplying a vector by a positive scalar greater than 1?
    [ ] It reverses the vector's direction
    [ ] It rotates the vector by 90°
    [CORRECT] It lengthens the vector's magnitude
    [ ] It collapses the vector to a point
- Q: Compute the scalar multiple 3<1, -2>.
    [ ] <3, -2>
    [CORRECT] <3, -6>
    [ ] <-3, 6>
    [ ] <1, -6>
- Q: The vector difference u - v is equivalent to which operation?
    [ ] Adding u to the vector v
    [ ] Multiplying the magnitudes of u and v
    [CORRECT] Adding u to the opposite of v
    [ ] Taking the midpoint of u and v
- Q: Compute the sum <2, 3> + <4, -1>.
    [CORRECT] <6, 2>
    [ ] <2, -3>
    [ ] <6, 4>
    [ ] <8, -3>
- Q: Compute the difference <5, 1> - <2, 4>.
    [ ] <-3, 3>
    [CORRECT] <3, -3>
    [ ] <3, 3>
    [ ] <7, 5>

### Vector word problems

- Q: A boat moves with velocity <6, 2> while the current adds velocity <1, -1>. What is the resulting velocity?
    [ ] <5, 3>
    [ ] <7, -1>
    [CORRECT] <7, 1>
    [ ] <6, 1>
- Q: A person walks 5 units east, then 2 units west. What is the net horizontal displacement?
    [ ] 7 units east
    [ ] 2 units east
    [CORRECT] 3 units east
    [ ] 3 units west
- Q: Why are vectors well suited to modeling motion problems?
    [ ] They record only the magnitude of motion
    [ ] They let you ignore the units involved
    [ ] They apply solely to physics contexts
    [CORRECT] They encode both magnitude and direction
- Q: A displacement of 3 units east and 4 units north is modeled by which vector (east = +x, north = +y)?
    [ ] <4, 3>
    [CORRECT] <3, 4>
    [ ] <3, -4>
    [ ] <-3, 4>
- Q: A force is given by the vector <3, 4>. What is its magnitude?
    [CORRECT] 5
    [ ] 7
    [ ] 4
    [ ] 12
