### The Binomial Theorem

- Q: Which type of expression does the Binomial Theorem expand?
    [ ] Only the difference $a - b$
    [ ] Only quadratic equations
    [ ] Only the sum $a^n + b^n$
    [CORRECT] A power of a binomial, $(a + b)^n$
- Q: In the expansion of $(a + b)^n$, what determines the numerical coefficients of the terms?
    [ ] Terms of a fixed arithmetic sequence with one constant gap
    [ ] Successive powers of 2 taken in strictly increasing order
    [ ] Successive prime numbers taken in strictly increasing order
    [CORRECT] Binomial coefficients read from rows of Pascal's triangle
- Q: Expand $(a + b)^3$.
    [ ] $a^3 + b^3$
    [CORRECT] $a^3 + 3a^2b + 3ab^2 + b^3$
    [ ] $a^3 + 2a^2b + 2ab^2 + b^3$
    [ ] $a^3 + 3ab + b^3$
- Q: What is the coefficient of the $x^2$ term in the expansion of $(x + 1)^3$?
    [ ] 1
    [ ] 4
    [ ] 2
    [CORRECT] 3
- Q: Expand $(x + 1)^2$.
    [ ] $2x^2 + 1$
    [ ] $x^2 + 1$
    [CORRECT] $x^2 + 2x + 1$
    [ ] $x^2 + x + 1$
