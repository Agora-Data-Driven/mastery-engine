- Q: Which of the following quantities is a statistic rather than a parameter?
    [ ] The standard deviation of the whole population
    [ ] The proportion in the entire population
    [CORRECT] The mean computed from a sample
    [ ] The mean of the entire population
- Q: A sampling distribution describes which of the following?
    [ ] The set of raw data values within a single sample
    [ ] The distribution of all individual values in the population
    [CORRECT] The distribution of a statistic over many repeated samples
    [ ] The joint distribution of two variables in a scatterplot
- Q: The center (mean) of the sampling distribution of the sample proportion p-hat equals
    [CORRECT] the population proportion p
    [ ] the sample size n itself
    [ ] the variance of p-hat only
    [ ] exactly 0.5 in every case
- Q: Why is the sampling distribution of a statistic useful?
    [ ] It eliminates the need to collect any sample data
    [ ] It removes all random error from the estimate
    [CORRECT] It shows how the statistic varies from sample to sample
    [ ] It proves the underlying population is normal
- Q: As the sample size increases, the sampling distribution of the sample mean generally becomes
    [ ] categorical rather than numeric
    [CORRECT] less spread out around its center
    [ ] more spread out around its center
    [ ] exactly identical regardless of n

    [CORRECT] less spread out
    [ ] more spread out

    [ ] 3
    [ ] 12

    [ ] 0.05
    [ ] 5
    [CORRECT] 0.5
    [ ] 50
- Q: If the population proportion is p = 0.5 and the sample size is n = 100, the standard deviation of p-hat is
    [CORRECT] 0.05
    [ ] 0.5
    [ ] 5
    [ ] 0.25
- Q: Holding the population proportion fixed, increasing the sample size n causes the standard deviation of p-hat to
    [ ] stay the same
    [CORRECT] decrease
    [ ] equal p
    [ ] increase
- Q: The standard deviation of the sampling distribution of $\hat{p}$ equals
    [ ] $\frac{p(1 - p)}{n}$
