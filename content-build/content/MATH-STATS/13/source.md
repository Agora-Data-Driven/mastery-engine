### Comparing two means (httpswww.khanacademy.org)

- Q: A two-sample t procedure for means is designed to compare which of the following?
    [ ] Two population variances
    [ ] One mean and one proportion
    [CORRECT] Two population means
    [ ] Two population proportions
- Q: A confidence interval for the difference of population means μ1 - μ2 contains 0. What does this indicate?
    [ ] There is strong evidence of a difference
    [ ] One of the samples must be biased
    [ ] The two means are exactly equal
    [CORRECT] There is no clear evidence of a difference
- Q: Two samples have means x-bar1 = 82 and x-bar2 = 77. What is the observed difference x-bar1 - x-bar2?
    [ ] 0.94
    [ ] 159
    [ ] -5
    [CORRECT] 5
- Q: What kind of response variable is being compared in a two-mean inference procedure?
    [ ] A binary yes/no outcome
    [CORRECT] A quantitative response variable
    [ ] A categorical response variable
    [ ] A count of distinct categories

### Comparing two proportions (httpswww.khanacademy.org)

- Q: A two-proportion z-test is used to compare
    [ ] two correlation values
    [ ] two standard deviations
    [ ] two population means
    [CORRECT] two population proportions
- Q: The sample statistics that drive a two-proportion z-test are
    [CORRECT] two sample proportions
    [ ] two sample medians
    [ ] two sample means
    [ ] a proportion and a mean
- Q: Which study feature best justifies comparing two proportions with a z-test?
    [ ] two groups whose underlying means match exactly
    [ ] an outcome variable with more than ten categories
    [CORRECT] independent random samples or random assignment
    [ ] sample sizes for the groups that are exactly equal
- Q: When a confidence interval for the difference p1 − p2 does not contain 0, this is evidence that
    [ ] there is no real difference
    [ ] the two samples are identical
    [ ] the two proportions add up to 1
    [CORRECT] the proportions truly differ
- Q: If two samples give p-hat1 = 0.60 and p-hat2 = 0.45, the observed difference p-hat1 − p-hat2 equals
    [ ] 0.27
    [ ] −0.15
    [CORRECT] 0.15
    [ ] 1.05
