### Analysis of variance (ANOVA) (khanacademy.org)

- Q: One-way ANOVA is used to compare:
    [ ] two population proportions
    [ ] exactly two population means
    [CORRECT] the means of three or more groups
    [ ] a set of standard deviations
- Q: In an ANOVA, a large value of the F statistic provides:
    [ ] evidence that all the group means are equal
    [CORRECT] evidence that at least one mean differs
    [ ] evidence that within-group spread is dominant
    [ ] evidence that no variability is present at all
- Q: What is the null hypothesis in a one-way ANOVA?
    [CORRECT] all of the group means are equal
    [ ] one mean exceeds all the others
    [ ] each sample mean equals its true value
    [ ] every group variance equals 1
- Q: The F statistic in one-way ANOVA is built by comparing:
    [ ] the regression slope to the line's intercept
    [CORRECT] between-group spread to within-group spread
    [ ] the observed cell counts to expected counts
    [ ] the mean of each group to its own median
- Q: If a one-way ANOVA test is statistically significant, the correct conclusion is that:
    [CORRECT] at least one group mean differs from the rest
    [ ] all of the underlying group variances are equal
    [ ] the largest observed sample mean is the true mean
    [ ] every group mean differs from every other one
