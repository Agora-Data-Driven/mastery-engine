### Law of sines

- Q: Which triangle configuration is especially well suited to solving with the Law of Sines?
    [ ] SSS (three sides)
    [ ] SAS (two sides, included angle)
    [ ] Right triangles only
    [CORRECT] AAS or ASA (two angles, one side)
- Q: In a triangle, if $A = 45^\circ$, $B = 45^\circ$, and $a = 8$, then $b$ equals what?
    [CORRECT] $8$
    [ ] $4$
    [ ] $16$
    [ ] $8\sqrt{2}$
- Q: In any triangle, the Law of Sines states a constant relationship between which quantities?
    [ ] Two sides and the included angle's sine
    [ ] Two sides and the tangent of an angle
    [ ] Each side and the cosine of its angle
    [CORRECT] Each side and the sine of its opposite angle
- Q: Using $\frac{a}{\sin(A)} = \frac{b}{\sin(B)}$ with $A = 30^\circ$, $a = 5$, and $B = 60^\circ$, what is $b$?
    [ ] $5$
    [ ] $10$
    [ ] $\frac{5}{\sqrt{3}}$
    [CORRECT] $5\sqrt{3}$
- Q: Why can the SSA case (two sides and a non-included angle) be ambiguous?
    [ ] It applies only to obtuse triangles
    [ ] The sine of an angle can be negative
    [ ] No valid triangle can ever be formed
    [CORRECT] Two different triangles may fit the data
