- Q: Use an angle addition identity to find the exact value of $\cos(15^\circ)$.
    [CORRECT] $\frac{\sqrt{6} + \sqrt{2}}{4}$
    [ ] $\frac{\sqrt{6} - \sqrt{2}}{4}$

- Q: What is the main use of the trigonometric angle addition identities?
