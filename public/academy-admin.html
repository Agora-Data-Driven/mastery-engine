Design a curriculum from my sources

Pick a folder — one module per folder is the tidy way — and the tree gets designed from what those sources teach, not from how they were packaged, so lessons are concepts and never “Week 3”. Tick Suggest gaps if you also want topics the subject needs but your sources miss — those come out empty, with no questions.
